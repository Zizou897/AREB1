Resy-Flex

Engineering a high-performance reservation platform tailored for the Ivorian hospitality market. Merging architectural rigidity with fluid user experiences.

Client Resy Group CI
Role Full-Stack Design & Dev
Timeline Q3 - Q4 2023
Stack Django, HTMX, Tailwind
02

Complexity
vs Ease

The Ivorian market presented a unique juxtaposition: high-volume, dynamic bookings demanding robust backend architecture, clashing with the necessity for absolute frontend simplicity due to varying levels of digital literacy among venue operators.

Previous solutions relied on fragmented WhatsApp chains and physical ledgers. The challenge was digitizing this workflow without introducing a steep learning curve. The interface needed to feel instantly familiar while abstracting the immense complexity of concurrent booking conflict resolution, localized payment gateways, and real-time availability sync.

03

Architectural
Rigidity

We bypassed heavy SPA frameworks in favor of a hyper-efficient Django + HTMX stack. This approach localized state management to the server, dramatically reducing client-side payload and ensuring rapid interactivity even on constrained cellular networks.

database

State Localization

Server-rendered templates infused with HTMX attributes for granular, localized DOM updates without full page reloads.

sync_alt

Concurrency

Optimistic locking and atomic transactions within PostgreSQL ensured zero double-bookings during peak Friday night rushes.
