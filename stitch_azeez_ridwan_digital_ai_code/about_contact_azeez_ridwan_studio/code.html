Role Lead Developer & UI/UX
Timeline 4 Months
Client Lumina Tech Group

Overview

Lumina required a platform that matched the premium quality of their physical products. The existing digital presence felt disconnected from their core brand values of precision and innovation. We needed to architect an experience that was not just functional, but viscerally engaging.

error_outline

The Challenge

Handling massive datasets for their product variations without compromising load times, while integrating a real-time WebGL configurator that needed to run flawlessly on mobile devices.

lightbulb

The Solution

A headless Shopify architecture powered by Next.js, utilizing a custom-built indexing service. We aggressively optimized the Three.js assets, implementing dynamic level-of-detail loading based on device capability.
